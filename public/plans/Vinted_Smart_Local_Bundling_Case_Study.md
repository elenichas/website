# Vinted Bundle Decisions

This plan supersedes the original speculative **Smart Local Bundling** concept. The filename is retained for existing links.

## Direction

Help buyers find useful items from one seller and understand the complete cost before deciding whether to bundle.

The original concept came first. Public Reddit research then tested its assumptions. A broad scan surfaced support recovery, search relevance and sizing confidence; a focused audit supported the existing bundling behaviour and challenged guaranteed savings and the local-distance framing.

## Research and design record

- Full research, limitations, pricing assumptions and evaluation protocol: `docs/vinted-bundle-research.md` in the repository.
- Source register and verbatim public excerpts: `src/data/vintedResearch.js`.
- Interactive case study: `/products/vinted-smart-bundling`.

## Retained and changed

Keep same-seller exploration. Add category and label-size controls. Show a complete, clearly labelled estimate and explain delivery changes. Compare a bundle against the original item with the same delivery method. Retain the option to buy only the original item.

Remove distance rankings, lower-impact claims, promised delivery dates, guaranteed savings and forced bundle purchasing. Bundles already exist on Vinted; this is an extension to the decision process.

## Current status

Research-informed independent concept, not affiliated with Vinted. The prototype uses fictional products, quotes and parcel weights. No participant tests, testing recordings, live experiment or impact measurements have been conducted. The scripted walkthrough demonstrates the interface only.

Success would be assessed through cost comprehension, relevant-item discovery and buyer control, followed by a properly powered live experiment if the feature were ever shipped. Larger baskets alone would not establish success.
