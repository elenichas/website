# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The primary audience is recruiters, hiring managers, and potential clients evaluating Eleni Chasioti's product design and engineering work. They need to understand her range quickly, inspect credible project detail, and decide whether to start a conversation or download her resume.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents product design, engineering, and spatial-computing work with enough clarity and depth to build confidence. It should make complex projects easy to browse while showing that Eleni can move from user needs and systems thinking to polished implementation. Success means a visitor can identify relevant work, understand Eleni's strengths, and take the next step by getting in touch or downloading her resume.

## Brand Personality

Professional, confident, creative. The tone should feel considered and capable, with enough personality to be memorable without becoming decorative or self-indulgent.

## Anti-references

Avoid AI slop websites: generic template compositions, repetitive card grids, gratuitous glassmorphism, purple or blue gradients, decorative effects without purpose, repeated tiny uppercase labels, and visual decisions that feel copied from a default portfolio generator. The target is a product designer's portfolio with a specific point of view.

## Design Principles

1. Show the work before describing the work.
2. Make complex systems legible without flattening their depth.
3. Use confident visual hierarchy so visitors can scan quickly and choose where to dive deeper.
4. Let creative moments demonstrate judgment, not compete with the projects.
5. Treat responsive behavior, accessibility, and implementation quality as part of the design.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA across the portfolio. Preserve keyboard access, visible focus states, semantic structure, readable contrast, meaningful alt text, and comfortable touch targets. Respect reduced-motion preferences throughout, especially for reveals, marquees, carousels, and animated playground interactions.
